# Black Souls

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every item you pick up from a chest, a sparkle on the ground, or a boss reward is replaced by an
item from the multiworld. Beating a boss is also a check.

The fights, rooms and companions are shuffled by the same engine as the standalone BLACK SOULS
randomizer.

- Boss Shuffle puts a different boss in each arena, scaled to the boss that normally lives there. Fairytale bosses, the ones that drop a fairytale book, trade only with each other and bring their book along, the rest trade among themselves. The Tortoise and Hare knights count as one boss
- Shuffle Final Bosses has Cinderella, Mary Sue, Baphomet (the Black Trial at the bottom of Kun-Yan), the Black Goat (the last fight of the Ending D route) and mimics trade arenas with the fairytale bosses. Every fairytale book then drops in the same place as in the normal game to prevent Ending C softlocks, so leave it off for a more random game
- Shuffle Demonic Princesses swaps the rooms of Princess Frog, Rapunzel, Snow White and the Little Mermaid. Each princess takes her own fight, covenant and book with her, and the logic follows her to her new room
- Shuffle Companions means that where you would meet one companion you get another instead, and she then waits at her usual Holy Forest spot. Leaf, Dorothy, Elma, Victoria, Goose, Jeanne, Miranda, Red Hood, Catherine and Elisabeth are all in it
- Shuffle the Prologue includes the prologue's pig fights and the dragon in Boss Shuffle and Enemy Shuffle, off keeps the opening as the normal game has it

Story progress stays tied to the arena, so beating whatever boss sits in Rapunzel's arena counts
as clearing Gothel's Tower.

## Which items are progression?

- Invitation to Atlantica opens the road from the Abandoned Beach to Atlantica
- With the Ending C goal, every fairytale book

The Lost Empire opens after the four princess rooms (Tainted Bog, Atlantica, Gothel's Tower and
Garden of Snow) have been cleared, the same as in the normal game.

## What is the goal?

The Goal option picks which ending finishes your game.

- Any Ending counts whichever ending you reach first
- Ending A and Ending B come from defeating Cinderella in the Lost Empire
- Ending C is the fairytale book route through Alice's Library. All 32 books go into the multiworld and Fairytale Books Needed sets how many Alice wants on her shelves before the lever works. The inner library room opens once that many minus 8 are shelved
- Ending D is the route through Kun-Yan, and the logic expects every area to be reachable before you finish

## Fewer checks

- Unique Checks Only makes one check per unique item, the way BLACK SOULS II does it. Weapons, rings, spells, key items and fairytale books each get one, every consumable spot gives its normal item. This takes the world from 600+ checks down to around 100, plus the boss kills
- Skip Kun-Yan Checks, Skip Lost Empire Checks and Skip Atlantica Checks leave those places out. Their pickups and bosses give their normal items. Kun-Yan is the Ending D dungeon, the underground world past the Tainted Bog

## What does another world's item look like in my game?

A popup in the bottom left shows the item name and who it belongs to.

## Notes

- NPC rewards that depend on story choices only hold filler, so a missed quest never locks you out
- The same goes for Dorothy's three targets, which never show up once she is locked away, and for Lamia
- Elisabeth, Jeanne, the Golden Goose, Marianna (the Vampire), the Crestfallen Hero (the Demonbeast Hero) and the Talking Flower (the Lord of Corruption) count their boss check however they die, so killing one early or in the basement still sends it
- Little Match Girl, the Demonbeast Hero and the Lord of Corruption only appear once all four princess rooms are cleared, and the logic expects that
- Shops are not randomized
- With Randomize Fairytale Books on, or the Ending C goal, bosses stop dropping their books and every book is somewhere in the multiworld. With it off, books stay where the normal game gives them
- The Pause Menu Checklist option adds a Checklist entry to the in-game pause menu while you are connected. It lists every check in your slot by area and ticks off the ones you have done
