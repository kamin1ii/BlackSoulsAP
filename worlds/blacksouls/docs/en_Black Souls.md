# Black Souls

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every item you pick up from a chest, a sparkle on the ground, or a boss reward is replaced by an
item from the multiworld. Beating a boss is also a check. With Boss Shuffle on, each boss arena
holds a different boss, scaled to the strength of the boss that normally lives there.

Story progress stays tied to the arena, so beating whatever boss sits in Rapunzel's arena counts
as clearing Gothel's Tower.

## Which items are progression?

- Master Key opens every locked door and is never used up
- Invitation to Atlantica opens the road from the Abandoned Beach to Atlantica

The Lost Empire opens after the four princess arenas (Tainted Bog, Atlantica, Gothel's Tower and
Garden of Snow) have been cleared, the same as in the normal game.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Cinderella at the top of the Lost Empire.

## What does another world's item look like in my game?

A popup in the bottom left shows the item name and who it belongs to.

## Notes

- NPC rewards that depend on story choices only hold filler, so a missed quest never locks you out
- Shops are not randomized
